All new employees are placed on a probation period of 6 months from their date of joining. During probation, the employee's performance, conduct, and suitability for the role are evaluated through regular check-ins with their manager. At the end of the probation period, a formal performance review is conducted to determine whether the employee will be confirmed as a permanent member of the organization. Employees who do not meet the expected standards during probation may have their probation extended or their employment terminated.
