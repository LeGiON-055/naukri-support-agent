Interviews are scheduled by the HR team after a candidate's application has been shortlisted, and the candidate is notified via email with the date, time, and format of the interview. If a candidate needs to reschedule, they must request the change at least 24 hours before the originally scheduled interview time. Rescheduling requests received less than 24 hours in advance may not be accommodated, and the interview slot may be forfeited. The HR team will confirm any rescheduled interview details via email.
